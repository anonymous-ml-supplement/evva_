# EVVA anonymous release — scoped scan

This reviewer-facing bundle has no prior-venue labels in its ZIP member names or text/binary payload according to the automated byte-pattern scan. The release's former supplementary filenames and the visible package documentation were neutralized. Existing experimental numbers and non-label scientific logic were not intentionally modified. The two key packaged evaluator hashes are release-local hashes after relabeling, not the historical draft-file hashes.

The scan checks ZIP names, UTF-8 text, script files, JSON, CSV, small pickle payload bytes, personal-home directory signatures, email address patterns, known author/institution strings, private key headers and simple credential-assignment signatures. Legitimate third-party package citations and public benchmark references are allowed. No scan can mathematically certify absence of all identifying information or secrets in binary data. The pickles are not unpickled by the scan.

For full counts, recorded command outcomes and any unresolved items see `docs/FINAL_VALIDATION.md`. Original experimental results and the final manuscript have not been edited by the packaging pass. End-to-end browser replay and absent candidate data are explicitly outside the supported validation.
